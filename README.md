# Postroom

A Mac app for planning and writing your posts, in your own voice.

Postroom keeps your ideas, drafts and posting plan in one place on your Mac. It helps you write for LinkedIn, YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and your blog, and it never posts anything for you: you copy the finished post and publish it yourself.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**

## What it does

- **Plan.** A calendar of what you're posting and when, with the posts you've already published shown on their dates.
- **Write.** An editor for each channel, with previews of how the post will look on desktop and on a phone.
- **Keep your voice.** A short guided setup captures how you write, who you write for and what you never say. Drafts are checked against it.
- **Ask for a draft.** Postroom can ask an AI assistant for a first draft or an edit. It uses the assistant you are already signed in to on your Mac (Claude Code or Codex), so there is no separate account and no extra bill from us. Nothing is applied until you approve it.
- **Collect ideas.** Save a rough thought, come back to it, archive what you won't use.
- **Use your own notes.** Point Postroom at a folder of notes, or a knowledge server you use, and drafts can draw on them.

## Install

1. Download `Postroom-<version>.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Unzip it and drag **Postroom** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app, because Postroom isn't distributed through the App Store. If it does, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the message about Postroom and choose **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.

**You need:** a Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 26 or later.

## Updates

Postroom checks this page for new versions by itself and tells you what changed. You can switch that off, or check by hand, in **Settings → Updates**.

Every download is signed. The app verifies an update before installing it and refuses one that doesn't match.

## Your data

- Your posts, ideas and settings are stored on your Mac, in your own user folder.
- Postroom has no account, no sign-up and no tracking.
- When you ask for a draft, the text goes to the assistant you chose, through its own app on your Mac, under your own sign-in. Postroom never sees your password or key.
- Postroom never publishes to any platform.
